A recurring deliverable across both Aurafloral and RB2F: a single source of truth for marketplace health — sales, pricing, inventory, and listing performance — refreshed automatically instead of stitched together by hand each week.

What it tracks
  • Revenue and unit sales by SKU, category, and marketplace (Amazon vs Flipkart), with week-over-week trend lines.
